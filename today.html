Savannah State Baseball Star Released From ICE Detention

JUSTICE & IMMIGRATION — Johan Sandoval, a Savannah State baseball player, was released from Federal Detention Center in Batavia on bond after being detained in Clifton Park at the beginning of July, and is now required to wear an ankle monitor. Administrative errors between Bryant & Stratton College and Savannah State University left Sandoval's student visa invalid when ICE detained him. A GoFundMe titled "Help Bring Johan Home to Finish His Final Year of College" has raised over $25,900 toward a $40,000 goal for court filings and legal fees.

The takeaway
What one lawyer calls a "bureaucratic mistake" has had catastrophic consequences for an accomplished student-athlete's future and brings into focus how easily HBCU students can get caught in immigration enforcement nets.
