SNAP Cuts Disproportionately Threaten Black Food Security

POLICY & JUSTICE — The "One Big, Beautiful Bill Act," signed into law in 2025, slashed $187 billion from the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program over the next decade, triggering new work requirements and stripping benefits from legal immigrants. The Center on Budget and Policy Priorities estimates that more than 1 million adults aged 55 and older will lose food assistance under the expanded rules, with Black Americans bearing disproportionate impact.

The takeaway
More than 4 million people have already lost SNAP since the law took effect, reshaping hunger across Black communities.
