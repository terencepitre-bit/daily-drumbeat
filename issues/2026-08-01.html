[ P2 · POLICY & JUSTICE · BRIEFING ]

Maryland Enacts Voting Rights Act to Protect Black Voters

POLICY & JUSTICE — Maryland's new Voting Rights Act bans racially discriminatory local voting systems and addresses longstanding barriers including unfair districts that weakened the power of Black voters. The legislation authorizes legal action for injunctive relief and damages against methods that impair voters' ability to elect candidates of their choice. Governor Wes Moore signed the measure into law, with the emergency legislation taking effect immediately.

The takeaway
Maryland becomes a model for state-level voting rights protection as the federal government weakens civil rights enforcement.
