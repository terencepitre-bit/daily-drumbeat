Howard Drops 500 Freshmen Over Tuition Deadline

EDUCATION CRISIS — Howard University sent notifications to 502 first-time-in-college students whose accounts did not reflect required enrollment and financial obligations by previously communicated deadlines. Many affected students argue they are still waiting on outside scholarships, military benefits or financial aid that isn't typically posted to student accounts until August 1. The unenrollments triggered outrage on social media and demands for resolution days before fall semester begins.

The takeaway
An elite HBCU's rigid payment deadline threatens to derail the college dreams of hundreds of low-income students with pending aid.

[ P2 · POLICY & JUSTICE · BRIEFING ]

D.C. Home Rule Under Fire as Congress Moves Tax Authority Bill

GOVERNMENT WATCH — The House Oversight Committee approved the Republican-backed D.C. Taxing Authority Review Act, which would require an affirmative vote from Congress before any new D.C. tax or fee increase could move forward. Congresswoman Eleanor Holmes Norton called it the most consequential reduction in D.C.'s authority to govern itself since the D.C. Home Rule Act was passed in 1973. The bill heads to the full House after passing committee on a party-line vote.

The takeaway
A GOP-backed measure to strip D.C. of tax authority signals a major attack on local control decades after home rule was won.
Sources [Washington Informer] [Eleanor Holmes Norton Official Statement]
[ P9 · HEALTH EQUITY · BRIEFING ]

Mississippi Woman's Death Reignites Push to Reopen Rural Hospital

HEALTH EQUITY — A pregnant woman suffering preeclampsia complications experienced a seizure while being rushed 25 miles to the nearest hospital after her local facility closed 13 years ago, dying along with her unborn daughter on the roadside. The county has lost a fourth of its population since the hospital closed, and leaders say healthcare services must be reestablished before growth can return.

The takeaway
Maternal death in Mississippi's healthcare desert exposes the deadly consequences of rural hospital closures in Black communities.
Sources [Capital B News] [KFF Health News]
