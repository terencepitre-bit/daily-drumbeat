Cori Bush Loses Primary Rematch to Wesley Bell

GOVERNMENT WATCH — Former Rep. Cori Bush lost her bid to retake her Missouri seat in Tuesday's Democratic primary, with incumbent Rep. Wesley Bell winning 59% of the vote compared to Bush's 37%. The loss further hurts Black women's chances for representation in Congress. Outside spending overwhelmingly favored Bell, with his campaign and allies outspending Bush by a staggering 27-to-1 ratio.

The takeaway
The race tested Democratic voters' appetite for Israel criticism at a time when AIPAC poured millions into the contest.
Sources [NBC News] [Missouri Independent]
[ P3 · ECONOMY & WORK · BRIEFING ]

Houston's 'Invisible Homelessness' Crisis Masks Racial Disparity

ECONOMY & WORK — An estimated 141,000 Houston-area residents experienced temporary homelessness over the past year, including those couch surfing, living in cars, or in short-term shelters. New data reveals racial disparities in these numbers, with Black Houstonians overrepresented; a city official emphasized that solving homelessness requires addressing Black empowerment and systemic racism.

The takeaway
The rate of temporary homelessness more than doubles for households earning under $50,000.
Sources [Houston Defender] [Houston Public Media]
[ P2 · POLICY & JUSTICE · BRIEFING ]

Federal Surge in D.C. Faces New Scrutiny Over Racial Prosecutions

JUSTICE & POLICY — A Black August rally highlighted allegations of federalized policing and unethical prosecutions in D.C. following a year of heightened enforcement. Speakers called out U.S. Attorney Jeanine Pirro for keeping the court lockup list near 100 and raising the D.C. jail population at least 18% higher than before the federal surge. According to court records, 90% of individuals on the lock-up list were Black.

The takeaway
The Trump administration reportedly ordered federal prosecutors in D.C. to be more aggressive, with offenses that would typically result in citations now resulting in criminal charges.
Sources [Washington Informer] [University of Colorado]
