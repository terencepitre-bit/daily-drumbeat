Missouri Man's Death in Police Custody Renews Accountability Demands

JUSTICE & ACCOUNTABILITY — Lloyd Gilmore, a 44-year-old Sikeston resident, died in police custody after an altercation with Sikeston police officers on June 10. His daughter Lania Gilmore said her father was looking for a safe space on the day he died, so he told her to call the police department for help. During a quick, physical altercation involving seven Sikeston police officers, he was shocked with a Taser and put into hobble restraints. Two autopsies were performed, but they — and Gilmore's cause of death — had not been released as of mid-July.

The takeaway
A man seeking mental health support died in custody, and answers remain months later—residents are demanding justice and systemic change.
