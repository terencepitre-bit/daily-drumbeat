Mississippi Woman's Death Demands Federal Scrutiny

JUSTICE WATCH — Tasia Fortune, 29, was identified Thursday after her body was found hanging from a tree behind a vacant Jackson, Mississippi home on Monday. Her city councilman sent a letter to federal prosecutors asking for assistance, emphasizing Mississippi's history of lynching. The investigation is ongoing with her body sent to state medical examiners for an autopsy.

The takeaway
Fortune's family and community leaders are demanding answers in a case that evokes Mississippi's darkest history.
