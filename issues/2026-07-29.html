Missouri Man's Death Renews Police Accountability Calls

POLICY & JUSTICE — Lloyd Gilmore, 44, died in Sikeston police custody on June 10 following a physical altercation involving seven officers. Police used a Taser and hobble restraints on Gilmore, who had mental health issues and sought police help. Two autopsies were performed but his cause of death has not been released, and toxicology results remain pending. His death fuels calls for accountability in a town with a history of racial violence.

The takeaway
Gilmore sought help at the police station but did not return home—renewing questions about police response to mental health crises.

[ P3 · ECONOMY & WORK · BRIEFING ]

Data Center Boom Compounds Rising Electricity Bills for Black Families

ECONOMY & WORK — Residential electricity rates have increased 18% despite Trump's promise to slash prices by 50% by July 2026, disproportionately affecting Black households, with more than 1 in 10 carrying overdue balances—three times the rate for white households. Power demand from data centers is expected to double by 2027 and quadruple by 2035. Utility executives warn blackouts could hit some regions as early as next year without investments in infrastructure.

The takeaway
AI data centers are reshaping electric grids—and forcing low-income Black families to choose between electricity, medicine, and food.
Sources [Capital B News] [Washington Informer]
[ P11 · BLACK EXCELLENCE · BRIEFING ]

Edwin Raymond Sworn in as NYC's First Haitian Sheriff

BLACK EXCELLENCE — Edwin Raymond was sworn in on July 27 as New York City sheriff, after his appointment by Mayor Mamdani on May 29. The son of Haitian immigrants took office on the same day Temporary Protected Status expired for more than 300,000 Haitians living in the U.S. Before his appointment, Raymond served in the New York State Attorney General's Office as the nation's first social justice liaison.

The takeaway
A former NYPD whistleblower and police reform advocate now leads the Sheriff's Office during a critical moment for immigrant communities.
Sources [NY Amsterdam News] [amNewYork]
