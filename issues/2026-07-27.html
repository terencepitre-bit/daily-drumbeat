Howard Drops 500 Freshmen Days Before Start of Fall Semester

HIGHER ED CRISIS — Howard University unenrolled 502 first-year students just weeks before fall semester begins August 17, citing missed tuition payment deadlines. Students and families say they communicated pending scholarships and financial aid that typically post in August. Many affected students are now scrambling to find alternative institutions as the university announced reviews but offered limited relief.

The takeaway
An HBCU heralded as a nurturing institution for Black students left hundreds in academic limbo, raising urgent questions about institutional accountability.
