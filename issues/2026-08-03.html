Karmelo Anthony Case Heads to Hearing on Judge Bias

POLICY & JUSTICE — Karmelo Anthony will return to court this month in his first step to overturn his murder conviction after being found guilty in the stabbing death of Austin Metcalf and sentenced to 35 years in prison. His new legal team has filed motions seeking the recusal of State District Judge John Roach Jr. from overseeing the appeals process, with two hearings scheduled for Aug. 19 and 20 in Collin County Courthouse in McKinney, Texas.

The takeaway
The case raises serious questions about judicial bias and fair trial guarantees in high-profile cases affecting young Black defendants.
Sources [Houston Defender] [2 Urban Girls]
[ P1 · BUSINESS & ENTERPRISE · BRIEFING ]

Pope Leo Reveals Mixed Ancestry in Slavery's Ledger

FAITH & LEGACY — Speaking to NBC News, Pope Leo XIV said his mother's family included both enslaved people and slaveholders—a striking admission from the first American pope who had previously declined to publicly discuss genealogical research tracing his ancestry to New Orleans' historic Creole community. The timing is especially significant because Leo has already demonstrated a willingness to confront slavery's legacy inside the Catholic Church, having issued the Vatican's most direct apology for its historic role in legitimizing slavery earlier this year, calling the church's record "a wound in Christian memory."

The takeaway
The pope's personal reckoning with America's racial history brings new moral weight to the Church's institutional apology for slavery.
Sources [AFRO American Newspapers] [Word In Black]
