Data Center Boom Doubles Electricity Costs for Black Families

ECONOMY & TECH — Despite Trump's pledge to slash electricity prices by 50% by July 2026, residential rates have actually increased 18%, disproportionately affecting Black households. Data center demand is driving rising utility bills and disconnections, with more than 1 in 10 Black households carrying overdue balances—three times the rate for white households.

The takeaway
As AI data centers proliferate, Black communities shoulder outsized energy costs while promised relief fails to materialize.

Georgia Special Election Today Tests Black Political Influence

POLICY & JUSTICE — Georgia's 13th Congressional District special election is being held today to fill the seat vacated by Democratic Rep. David Scott's death in April. The heavily Democratic district gave Harris 71% support in 2024 versus 28% for Trump, making it a bellwether for Black voter turnout and Democratic control.

The takeaway
Today's election offers a test of Black political power in a district where Scott championed voting rights and agricultural equity for decades.
